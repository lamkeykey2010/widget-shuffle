THE SMALL PRINT

Grow an app into a widget.

  1. Every icon has a number. That is its widget's area in grid spaces.
  2. Drag between two corners to draw a rectangle. You can also tap the first corner, then the opposite corner.
  3. Each rectangle must contain exactly one app icon and match its number.
  4. Fill the screen without overlaps or gaps. Widgets reveal their content as they expand.

Change your mind? Hover over a widget and select its centre Shrink button. On touch, tap the widget to reveal the button. It returns that app to its original icon. Undo brings it back.

Keyboard: use Tab or the arrow keys to move between cells. Press Enter or Space on two corners. Press Delete on an occupied cell to shrink that widget, or Tab to its Shrink button. Escape cancels a selection.
